# **Gachiakiuta**
---
###  The plot of Gachiakuta follows Rudo, a young boy framed for murder in a floating city who is exiled to the *"Abyss"*—a giant junkyard below where trash and people are dumped. In this wasteland, he must fight monsters made of trash, known as *"aberrant beasts,"* with the help of *"jinki"* or special weapons and join an organization of *"Cleaners"* or *"Givers"* to survive and seek revenge. The series explores themes of social class and corruption as Rudo navigates this new world, seeking the truth about his father's death and his own origins.
